Nature’s calm, poured by hand.

Gelamé started in 2026 with one small idea: a candle should make the end of your day feel quieter.

Where it began

Gelamé is a small Malaysian candle studio. We make every candle by hand, in small batches, because we want each jar to feel looked after, not mass produced.

Our first scent, Gelam, is the one we are proudest of. It is the signature of the brand, and the reason the name sits on every label.

We want our candles to belong in ordinary corners of your life: the study table, the bedside, the end of a long day.

What goes in the jar

Nothing complicated. Soy wax, fine fragrance oils, a cotton wick, and an amber glass jar that keeps the light warm.
